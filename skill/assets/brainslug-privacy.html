Brainslug Privacy Notice

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Brainslug is a private beta Alexa skill that answers general-knowledge questions and can repeat the most recent saved answer for an Alexa account.

Information Brainslug processes

When you use Brainslug, Amazon Alexa sends the skill an Alexa-assigned account identifier, a session identifier, and the text Alexa recognized from your request. Brainslug may store those identifiers, your question, the generated short and full answers, the provider and model names, timestamps, latency, request status, and error details in a local SQLite database.

Alexa-assigned identifiers do not provide Brainslug with your name, email address, telephone number, or voice recording. Do not include sensitive personal information in questions submitted to the skill.

How the information is used

The information is used to answer your question, let the same Alexa account request its latest saved answer, operate the beta, and diagnose failures. During this beta, questions are processed by the locally hosted language model selected by the skill operator.

Sharing and sales

Brainslug does not sell personal information, display advertising, support purchases, or use Alexa Shopping Actions. Data is not shared with third parties for advertising. Amazon processes Alexa requests under Amazon's own privacy terms.

Storage, retention, and security

Brainslug stores history on the skill operator's local system. The history and administrative interface are not intentionally exposed through the public Alexa endpoint. Beta records are retained until the operator deletes them; automated retention and self-service deletion are not currently available.

Deletion and questions

Disabling the skill does not automatically erase previously stored history. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of your beta history, contact the person who invited you to the Brainslug beta and identify the Alexa account used for testing without sending passwords or authentication codes.

Children

Brainslug is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the beta's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
